# The vocabulary the authored pages write in, and nothing else. A word belongs here when it is a
# term of art, an identifier quoted from source or from `microvm manifest`, or a dependency's name,
# never because a spelling was inconvenient to fix. The pages write US English: `cspell.json`
# carries a `flagWords` list that rejects the recurring British forms (`behaviour`, `colour`,
# `catalogue`) with their US replacements.
#
# cspell splits on case and on `-`/`_`/`.`, so a compound like `runHookPayload` needs no entry and
# `microvms-core` needs only `microvms`.
#
# Only `site/authored/**` is checked. The `docs/` tree is a generated codebase-understanding pass
# whose edits ccu discards, and the Reference tier is generated from `docs/manifest.json`.
